## 0. Honest framing first

- **Awards ≠ money.** Almost no award gives cash or resources — they give a badge + press. For RoboParts the
  realistic value is **marketing credibility**, not funding. Judge any award by "does this badge help us sell /
  recruit / partner?" not "will this pay the bills?".
- **RoboParts is infrastructure, not a robot.** The robotics-award world is product-centric (deployed robots,
  hardware, physical innovation). A cross-vendor *mechanical-interface compatibility data layer* fits only a
  narrow slice of categories (software / platform / data / web / AI).
- **"No travel" is fine for most.** Winning rarely requires attending the gala. The awards that *force* on-site
  presence (VDMA finals in Berlin) are excluded below.
- **Beware vanity awards.** A large share of "Global / International Robotics Awards" are pay-to-win lead-gen
  operations: pay a fee, get a trophy, no real jury. Only touch awards with clear industry backing
  (IFR/IEEE, The Robot Report, Webby, Deutsche Messe, VDMA).

## 2. Shortlist & recommended path

1. **RBR50 2027 — do this (zero cost, authoritative, no travel).**
   - **Host & tier:** The Robot Report (WTWH Media), running since 2012 (15th edition). The most credible
     *commercial/technical* robotics award — Chinese trade press calls it the "robotics Oscars". Judged by a panel of
     front-line robotics engineers, not a popular vote. Past winners are all industry leaders: Amazon (Vulcan),
     ABB, Boston Dynamics, NVIDIA, KUKA, Universal Robots, Geek+ (5× winner), Physical Intelligence, Harvard, Waymo,
     Agility Robotics. **This is an industry/business award, NOT an academic one.**
   - **Real difficulty (honest):** the *submission* is simple — a free online form (company info + logo + 500-word
     "describe your innovation" + 300-word "why it's a contender" + a proof link). No travel, no fee, no forced
     gala attendance. BUT **win-odds for our profile are low**: RBR50 favors *deployed, real-world-impact robots*
     with hard numbers (2026 winner Vulcan processed 500k orders; Geek+ deployed at Schneider Electric). RoboParts
     is a young *data/web platform* with no on-site robot and small user base — our best-fit categories are
     *Business* (open-data milestone / business model) or *Product* (enabling technology / data infrastructure).
     Treat it as a zero-cost credibility bet, not a likely win.
   - **Window:** the 2027 round honors innovations *initiated/released/executed in the 2026 calendar year*. Our
     compatibility data layer + 13-step regen + 9-flange PCD matrix all shipped in 2026 → on-window. Nominations
     open ~Nov/Dec 2026, close ~mid-Dec 2026, gala May 2027 in Boston. **You cannot submit yet (it's 2026-09)** —
     use the gap to prep the narrative.
   - Frame our 2026 milestone: *"An open cross-vendor robot mechanical-interface compatibility engine + 770-entity
     dataset, with a 9-flange ISO 9409-1 PCD matrix proving 100% adapter-required and a one-command derived-artifact
     regen pipeline."* That is a concrete, defensible "innovation" story.
   - Category: *Products* (enabling technology / research) or *Business* (open-data business model).
2. **Webby 31st — optional, only if budget allows the entry fee.**
   - Enter *Websites* (data/reference) or *AI* (copilot/compat features). Prep a tight 500-word "what + why it's
     contender" + live-site proof. Accept ~low odds given volume.
3. **World Tech Awards — hold, verify prestige before any payment.** If it proves to have a real independent jury,
   it's a cheap, robotics-labeled entry; if not, skip as vanity.

## 3. What to prepare now (so we're ready at each window)

- One-page innovation narrative (English): problem (adapter tax / no open compat layer) → approach (data + engine +
  PCD matrix) → evidence (770 entities, regen pipeline, 81 negative-compat rules) → impact (lowers EU/Asia SME
  integration cost).
- A "launched in 2026" anchor: the public dataset + copilot page + regen pipeline all shipped this year — cite dates.
- 3–5 high-res screenshots / a 90s demo clip of the compat engine and copilot (for Webby / any entry needing assets).
- Keep the Open Source story crisp: code MIT / data CC BY 4.0 (helps any "open" angle without re-opening the grant line).

## 4. Vanity-award red flags (do NOT engage)

- "Nominate your company — you've been selected!" unsolicited emails.
- Fees with no named, independent jury; trophies shipped to everyone who pays.
- Pressure deadlines ("award closes Friday").
- No prior credible winners listed.

## 5. Bottom line

The only **low-cost, credible, no-travel** award that fits a young robotics *data platform* is **RBR50 (self-nomination,
free, 2027 window)**. Webby is credible but paid + competitive. Everything else is either a poor fit (robot-product
awards), requires travel (VDMA), already closed, or unverified prestige. Recommend: pursue RBR50 2027, keep Webby as
a maybe, skip the rest. This matches the project's "zero-cost-first" discipline.
