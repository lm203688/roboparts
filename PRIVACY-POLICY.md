# Privacy Policy — RoboParts

> Effective Date: November 8, 2026  
> Last Updated: November 8, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 User-Provided Information
- **Account Information**: When you create an account, we collect your email address and profile information.
- **Usage Data**: We collect information about how you interact with our services, including API requests, search queries, and feature usage.
- **Technical Data**: Device information, browser type, IP address, and usage patterns.

### 1.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Server Logs**: IP addresses, browser type, operating system, referring pages, and timestamps.
- **API Usage Statistics**: Request frequency, endpoints accessed, and response times.
- **Error Reports**: Application errors and performance metrics.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Primary Purposes
- **Service Delivery**: To provide and maintain our robot parts compatibility checking service.
- **Improvement**: To analyze usage patterns and improve our algorithms and user experience.
- **Security**: To protect our systems and users from abuse and unauthorized access.
- **Research**: To advance robotics research and open-source data standards.

### 2.2 Secondary Purposes
- **Analytics**: To understand how our service is used and identify opportunities for improvement.
- **Compliance**: To comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 3.1 Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following cases:

- **Service Providers**: With vendors who help us operate our service (e.g., cloud infrastructure providers).
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
- **Research Partners**: With academic and research institutions for non-commercial research purposes.

### 3.2 Open-Source Data
All API data is published under CC BY 4.0 license and is freely available for public use.

## 4. Data Security

### 4.1 Protective Measures
- **Encryption**: Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
- **Access Controls**: Strict access controls and authentication mechanisms.
- **Regular Audits**: Regular security assessments and penetration testing.
- **Data Minimization**: Only collect and retain information necessary for our services.

### 4.2 Incident Response
In the event of a security breach, we will:
- Notify affected users within 72 hours
- Take immediate steps to contain the breach
- Conduct a thorough investigation
- Implement preventive measures

## 5. User Rights

### 5.1 Data Access and Control
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal information.
- **Correction**: Update or correct inaccurate information.
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your personal information.
- **Portability**: Obtain your data in a portable format.

### 5.2 Exercise Your Rights
To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@roboparts.cc.

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods
- **Account Data**: Until account deletion or 5 years of inactivity.
- **Usage Data**: 2 years for analytics purposes.
- **Server Logs**: 30 days for security purposes.
- **API Response Caches**: 24 hours for performance optimization.

### 6.2 Data Deletion
We will permanently delete your data upon request, except where required by law to retain certain records.

## 7. International Data Transfers

Our service operates primarily from China. When you use our service, your data may be processed in jurisdictions with different data protection laws. We take appropriate measures to ensure your data remains protected.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will:
- Notify users of material changes via email or in-app notification
- Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of the policy
- Provide a transition period for significant changes
