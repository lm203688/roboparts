Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript titled \emph{``Three-State
Honesty in Robotic Hardware Compatibility: A Vendor-Neutral
Determination Layer''} for consideration for publication as a Regular
Article in \emph{Mechatronics: The Science of Intelligent Machines}.

The manuscript reports an original formalisation of hardware
compatibility as a three-valued function
$\mathsf{compose}(a,b) \in \{\mathsf{composed}, \mathsf{type\_error},
\mathsf{unknown}\}$ with \emph{unknown} taking priority over
\emph{composed}. This is instantiated as \textbf{RoboParts}, a
vendor-neutral determination layer evaluated on $802$ real-world
robotic entities, yielding $351{,}649$ composite pair evaluations.

We believe the manuscript fits the aims and scope of \emph{Mechatronics}
in three respects. First, our work sits squarely at the mechatronics
intersection of mechanical specification, electrical interfaces and
signal semantics, and provides a unified type-system treatment across
the three axes. Second, the paper reports real experimental results
on a real dataset with explicit reproducibility artifacts
(open-source code under MIT and data under CC BY 4.0 at
\url{https://github.com/lm203688/roboparts}). Third, the reflexivity
boundary between geometric-specification and directional-role types is
a small but safety-critical formal contribution that generalises
beyond our corpus.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, is not under consideration
elsewhere, and that all authors have approved the submission. No
competing interests exist. Generative-AI use is confined to language
editing and is disclosed in the manuscript.

We have no reviewer suggestions at this time and will leave reviewer
assignment to the editors.

Thank you for considering our manuscript.
