# Open Collective 申请简介（粘贴到 opencollective.com 创建 Collective）

**Name:** RoboParts
**Short description:** Open compatibility dataset & matching engine for humanoid/bionic robot parts — "PCPartPicker for robots".
**Longer description:**
RoboParts catalogs robot parts (actuators, joints, mounts, connectors, sensors) with machine-readable mechanical/electrical interface specs, and runs a compatibility engine that tells builders what fits what before they buy. 708 parts across 11 categories, openly licensed (CC BY 4.0), published on ModelScope. The goal is shared open infrastructure for the growing open humanoid DIY ecosystem (Asimov, ROBOTIS AI Sapiens, Berkeley Humanoid Lite, OpenFigure, ROS 2 builders).

**What the money funds:** ongoing ingestion of new parts, maintenance of the compatibility engine, raising the mechanical-interface declaration rate (currently 1.68%), and keeping the dataset + API free and open.

**Why Open Collective (no entity):** we're a pre-revenue open-source project without a legal entity; Open Source Collective fiscal hosting lets us receive transparent backer/sponsor funds without incorporating.
